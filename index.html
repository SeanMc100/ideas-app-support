Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. Ideas doesn't have an account, doesn't make network requests, and doesn't collect analytics — see the privacy policy for the full detail.
I deleted the app — can I get my ideas back?
Not currently. Since everything lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes its data with it. iCloud sync (so your library survives a reinstall and stays in step across your devices) is planned.
Why doesn't the app show my newest idea first?
By design. The deck always opens on the idea you've gone longest without seeing — that's what makes old ideas resurface without a queue, streak, or algorithm you have to think about.
Something isn't working right.
Email the address above with what happened and, if you can, what device and iOS version you're on.
