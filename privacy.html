Privacy Policy

Ideas is built to hold what you write and nothing else.

Summary

  • Ideas does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data.
  • Everything you write stays on your device.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • The app makes no network requests.

What the app stores

Ideas uses Apple's SwiftData framework to store your cards — the idea or quote you capture, your reflections on it, and any optional source — entirely in a local database on your device. This data is never sent anywhere by the app.

iCloud sync

Ideas does not currently sync your data anywhere. If iCloud sync is enabled in a future version, it will use Apple's CloudKit service to sync your cards across your own devices, under your own iCloud account. That data would be transmitted only to Apple's iCloud, governed by Apple's iCloud privacy practices, and would never be accessible to the developer. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Third parties

Ideas does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. No data is shared with any third party, because no data leaves your device.

Data deletion

Since all data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes all your data. There is no server-side copy to separately request deletion of.

Children's privacy

Ideas does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, when iCloud sync is introduced — this document will be updated and the date below revised.
